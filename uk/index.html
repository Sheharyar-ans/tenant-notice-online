Frequently Asked UK Tenancy Questions

Are Section 21 no-fault evictions still lawful in England?

No. The Renters' Rights Act has completely abolished Section 21 no-fault evictions across England and Wales. Private landlords must now cite and substantiate reformed grounds under Section 8 of the Housing Act 1988.

What is the notice period for serious rent arrears under reformed Ground 8?

Under the reformed Housing Act 1988, mandatory Ground 8 requires the tenant to owe at least 3 months of unpaid rent (increased from 2 months) both at the date of notice service and the court hearing. The notice period is 4 weeks.

How much notice must a tenant give to end a periodic tenancy?

Tenants on statutory periodic rolling tenancies have the right to end their tenancy by providing 2 calendar months written notice, expiring on the final day of a rental period.
