Statutory Limitations & Local Ordinances

Residential landlord-tenant law is subject to intense local variation. While our tools cite primary governing state codes (such as California Civil Code, Texas Property Code, and Florida Statutes) and parliamentary enactments (such as the Housing Act 1988 as reformed), local city ordinances may impose stricter prerequisites.

For example, municipal rent stabilization ordinances in cities such as San Francisco, Los Angeles, or New York City may prohibit eviction without specific enumerated just cause, require registration with local rent boards, or mandate substantial tenant relocation payments before a notice to vacate may take legal effect. Similarly, localized licensing rules in the United Kingdom (such as selective licensing schemes) can invalidate possession notices served by unlicensed landlords.
