# Humanizer Editorial Protocol (blader/humanizer)

## Purpose
The `humanizer` skill provides an uncompromising editorial framework designed to transform synthetic, AI-generated text into authoritative, rhythmically varied, authentic human prose. It systematically eliminates all 26 common AI writing tells, strips out machine clichés, and bans formatting artifacts like em-dashes.

---

## 1. Absolute Formatting Bans
- **Zero Em-Dashes (`—`):** Absolutely no em-dashes across titles, meta tags, body prose, FAQs, or templates. Replace strictly with standard commas, periods, colons, or parentheses.
- **Zero Double-Hyphens (`--`):** Replace with proper punctuation.
- **Zero Faux-Sophisticated Bulleted Lists:** Avoid turning simple explanations into unnatural bullet point soup.

---

## 2. The 26 Common AI Writing Patterns to Purge

1. **Staged Run-Ups:**
   - *Banned:* "When it comes to tenancy...", "In the realm of property law...", "It is essential to understand that..."
   - *Fix:* State the core subject immediately and directly.
2. **"Not X, but Y" Rhetorical Formulations:**
   - *Banned:* "This is not just a notice; it is a statutory defense.", "It's not about winning, but about compliance."
   - *Fix:* State what it is without setting up a synthetic contrast.
3. **Forced Triads (Rule of Three Addiction):**
   - *Banned:* "Transparent, efficient, and legally binding", "accidental, unintentional, and avoidable"
   - *Fix:* Use two precise descriptors or a single accurate adjective.
4. **Dramatic One-Line Closers & Faux Profundity:**
   - *Banned:* "And that makes all the difference.", "The stakes have never been higher.", "In the end, knowledge is power."
   - *Fix:* Conclude with a concrete procedural step or factual summary.
5. **Stock AI Vocabulary & Buzzwords:**
   - *Banned:* `delve`, `delve into`, `tapestry`, `in today's fast-paced world`, `furthermore`, `testament to`, `a beacon of`, `navigating the complexities`, `robust`, `seamless`, `seamlessly`, `plethora`, `myriad`, `vital`, `crucial`, `pivotal`, `cornerstone`, `leverage`, `empower`, `game-changer`, `foster`, `holistic`.
   - *Fix:* Use plain, precise language (e.g., use "examine" or "review" instead of "delve", "use" instead of "leverage", "effective" instead of "robust").
6. **Rhetorical Questions as Section Transitions:**
   - *Banned:* "Why does this matter?", "What should a landlord do next?", "How does this impact you?"
   - *Fix:* Use descriptive, informative subheadings and direct introductory sentences.
7. **Monotonous Sentence Cadence:**
   - *Banned:* Strings of medium-length compound sentences with identical grammatical structures.
   - *Fix:* Vary sentence rhythm. Follow a 25-word complex explanation with a crisp 6-word assertion.
8. **Summary-Hungry Paragraph Endings:**
   - *Banned:* Ending every paragraph with a hollow recap sentence starting with "Ultimately", "Overall", or "In summary".
   - *Fix:* Let the evidence stand. Move immediately to the next substantive point.
9. **Synonym Spinning & Over-Decoration:**
   - *Banned:* Using elaborate synonyms for straightforward concepts ("domicile" instead of "home", "monetary outlay" instead of "fee").
   - *Fix:* Use the standard industry or legal term consistently.
10. **False Neutrality & Bothsidesism:**
    - *Banned:* "While some may argue X, others feel Y is equally valid, leading to varied viewpoints."
    - *Fix:* Provide clear, evidence-backed statutory facts and judicial requirements.
11. **Metaphor Overdrive:**
    - *Banned:* Treating legal or technical processes as "journeys", "roadmaps", "symphonies", or "uncharted waters".
    - *Fix:* Stick to literal, domain-specific terminology.
12. **Passive Shielding:**
    - *Banned:* "It should be noted that mistakes are often made..."
    - *Fix:* "Tenants often miscalculate statutory notice periods..."
13. **Unearned Emotionalism:**
    - *Banned:* "Navigating an eviction is a deeply terrifying ordeal that tests one's spirit."
    - *Fix:* Address the legal and financial reality with calm, objective professionalism.
14. **Over-Hedged Assertions:**
    - *Banned:* "It could potentially be argued that courts might possibly view..."
    - *Fix:* "Courts consistently reject..."
15. **Robotic Symmetry:**
    - *Banned:* Every section having identical length, 3 bullet points, and an identical conclusion structure.
    - *Fix:* Let the depth of each topic dictate the length of the section naturally.
16. **Hollow Attributions:**
    - *Banned:* "Experts agree that...", "Industry leaders suggest..."
    - *Fix:* Cite specific statutes, judicial precedents, official government guidance, or legal sections.
17. **Conversational Filler & Preamble:**
    - *Banned:* "Let's take a closer look at...", "Now, we will explore..."
    - *Fix:* Remove filler entirely.
18. **Dramatic Intensifiers:**
    - *Banned:* `truly`, `fundamentally`, `undeniably`, `categorically`, `massively`.
    - *Fix:* Delete the intensifier; let the factual assertion carry its own weight.
19. **Formulaic Introductions:**
    - *Banned:* Starting with dictionary definitions ("According to Merriam-Webster...") or temporal clichés ("Since the dawn of time...").
    - *Fix:* Start directly with the operative statute, real-world trigger, or core problem.
20. **Echo Transitions:**
    - *Banned:* Repeating the last sentence of section A as the first sentence of section B.
    - *Fix:* Move forward with new information.
21. **Artificial Urgency:**
    - *Banned:* "Act now before it is too late!", "Every second counts!"
    - *Fix:* State statutory deadlines clearly (e.g., "Texas Property Code § 24.005 requires a minimum 3-day notice").
22. **Generic Takeaways:**
    - *Banned:* "Remember to always do your homework and stay informed."
    - *Fix:* Provide actionable checklists with statutory citations and filing steps.
23. **Stock Anthropomorphism:**
    - *Banned:* "The law breathes life into tenant rights."
    - *Fix:* "The statute establishes tenant protections."
24. **Sycophantic Tone:**
    - *Banned:* Flattering the reader or showering standard procedures in praise.
    - *Fix:* Keep tone neutral, objective, and authoritative.
25. **Superlative Inflation:**
    - *Banned:* "The most revolutionary change in residential leasing history."
    - *Fix:* "A major statutory reform altering tenancy structure."
26. **Synthetic Symmetry in Pros/Cons:**
    - *Banned:* Creating artificial counterarguments just to balance word count.
    - *Fix:* Present factual realities as they exist under statutory law.

---

## 3. Human Cadence & Tone Calibration
- **Cadence:** Alternate between short declarative sentences and detailed, multi-clause statutory breakdowns.
- **Voice:** Knowledgeable legal-tech practitioner, objective court clerk, or specialized paralegal.
- **Direct Answers:** Provide factual answers up front within the first 45 words for SearchGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.
- **Integrity Shield:** Never compromise statutory citations, legal section numbers, court timelines, or technical precision when rewriting for human tone.
