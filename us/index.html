Frequently Asked US Tenancy Questions

How do US notice requirements differ between states?

Each US state codifies distinct cure periods and service rules. California requires 3 days excluding weekends and judicial holidays for rent nonpayment under CCP Section 1161(2), Texas permits 3-day or lease-shortened 24-hour notices, and Florida mandates 3 days under Florida Statutes Section 83.56(3).

Can a landlord terminate a month-to-month lease without cause in California?

Under the California Tenant Protection Act of 2019 (Civil Code Section 1946.2), tenancies subject to state rent control require legitimate at-fault or no-fault just cause once tenants occupy the premises for 12 months or longer.

What is the penalty for withholding a security deposit in Texas?

Texas Property Code Section 92.109 imposes a statutory penalty of 100 dollars plus three times the unlawfully withheld deposit amount, in addition to reasonable attorney fees, for landlords who fail to return deposits within 30 days in bad faith.
