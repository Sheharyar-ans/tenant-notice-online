Frequently Asked Questions

Are notices generated on TenantNotice legally binding?

Yes. TenantNotice drafts notices following exact statutory civil codes in the US (such as California Civil Code Section 1946.1) and reformed parliamentary grounds under the UK Renters' Rights Act, provided they are served in strict accordance with statutory service procedures.

Why are United States and United Kingdom portals separated?

US and UK tenancy laws operate on fundamentally contradictory statutory concepts. Isolating jurisdictions into dedicated subdirectories eliminates cross-border confusion, guarantees local code compliance, and prevents fatal court dismissals.

Does TenantNotice store my personal tenancy or lease information?

No. TenantNotice is architected with a zero-data-retention client-side engine. All document compilation occurs locally inside your browser memory. No names, addresses, or monetary amounts are ever sent to our servers.
